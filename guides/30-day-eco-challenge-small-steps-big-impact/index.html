30-Day Eco-Challenge: Small Steps, Big Impact

(Image: A simple, friendly illustration of a green leaf with a checkmark inside)

Goal: Reduce your environmental impact, one small step at a time. This challenge focuses on achievable actions for a healthier planet.

---

Week 1: Reducing Waste

  • Day 1: - [ ] Bring your own reusable shopping bag.
  • Day 2: - [ ] Say no to a plastic straw.
  • Day 3: - [ ] Compost food scraps (if possible).
  • Day 4: - [ ] Reduce food waste – plan your meals.
  • Day 5: - [ ] Choose products with minimal packaging.
  • Day 6: - [ ] Recycle paper, plastic, and glass.
  • Day 7: - [ ] Swap out disposable napkins for cloth ones.

---

Week 2: Conserving Resources

  • Day 8: - [ ] Take shorter showers.
  • Day 9: - [ ] Turn off lights when leaving a room.
  • Day 10: - [ ] Unplug electronics when not in use.
  • Day 11: - [ ] Wash clothes in cold water.
  • Day 12: - [ ] Line dry clothes instead of using a dryer.
  • Day 13: - [ ] Reduce your water usage while brushing teeth.
  • Day 14: - [ ] Adjust your thermostat – lower it in winter, raise it in summer.

---

Week 3: Sustainable Choices

  • Day 15: - [ ] Choose to walk, bike, or take public transport.
  • Day 16: - [ ] Support local businesses.
  • Day 17: - [ ] Reduce your meat consumption (one meatless meal).
  • Day 18: - [ ] Buy secondhand items.
  • Day 19: - [ ] Choose reusable containers for leftovers.
  • Day 20: - [ ] Plant a seed or small plant.
  • Day 21: - [ ] Learn about a local environmental issue.
